Session tone classification — based on clinical reactivity, not demeanor
Positive — PEMF ≤ 1.5 and ≤ 2 reactive areas. Minimal fasciculations, low palpation sensitivity.
Mixed — PEMF 2.0 or 3+ reactive areas. Moderate fasciculations present; may feel good overall but body is still reactive.
Concern — PEMF 2.5+ or explicit soreness noted. Significant fasciculations, multiple reactive areas, or therapist flags specific findings.
